# About VeloRix Tournaments

VeloRix Tournaments is a mobile esports tournament platform built in India, for Indian mobile gamers. We run daily custom-room tournaments for Free Fire, BGMI, Call of Duty Mobile and Clash Squad, and we pay real cash prizes to verified winners. The platform exists because most grassroots tournaments in India are organised informally over chat groups: schedules slip, room IDs leak, results are disputed and payouts often never arrive. VeloRix replaces that with a structured, reviewable process.

## What we do

- Publish a daily tournament schedule with clear formats, entry fees (many are free) and prize splits.
- Distribute custom room IDs and passwords only to registered players, close to match time.
- Review results with in-house anti-cheat signals plus manual review of flagged matches.
- Pay verified winners after the review is complete, with a record of the decision.

## Fair play

Cheating is the fastest way to kill a competitive community, so detection is not optional for us. We combine automated signals (impossible statistics, device and account anomalies, repeated pattern flags) with human review of clips and lobby records. Bans are applied to accounts and devices. Appeals are handled over email.

## Built for real devices

Most of our players are on budget and mid-range Android phones. The app targets Android 7.0 and newer, weighs about 25 MB, and is deliberately built light so that lobbies, registrations and result screens still work on slow connections and older hardware.
