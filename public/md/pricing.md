# VeloRix Tournaments — Pricing and Fees

Plain-language pricing for agents and users. Last reviewed: 2026-08-23.

## App and website

- VeloRix Android app: free to download, no subscription, no paid tiers.
- Website account: not required. Downloads and all public pages are open.
- Requirements: Android 7.0 or newer, roughly 25 MB install size.

## Tournament entry

- Free-entry tournaments run daily. No payment of any kind is required to compete in these.
- Paid-entry tournaments carry a small per-player entry fee, shown on the tournament card before you register. Typical range is ₹10 to ₹100 per player, depending on prize pool and slot count.
- Entry fees are always displayed before registration. VeloRix does not charge hidden or recurring fees.
- Squad tournaments charge per player unless the tournament explicitly states a per-team fee.

## Prizes and payouts

- Prize pools are published on each tournament before registration opens.
- Winners are paid in cash after result verification and anti-cheat review.
- Payouts go to the verified payout details on the player's app account.
- VeloRix keeps a stated platform share of paid tournament entry fees to cover operations; the prize split is written on the tournament page.

## Refunds

- If a tournament is cancelled by VeloRix, entry fees for that tournament are refunded.
- If a player is disqualified for cheating or rule violations, the entry fee is not refunded.
- Disputes go through the support channel at service.veloxyra@gmail.com within the dispute window stated in the tournament rules.

## What VeloRix is not

VeloRix runs skill-based competitive matches only. It is not betting, gambling, wagering, fantasy staking, or an account marketplace. No product on VeloRix offers a guaranteed monetary return.
