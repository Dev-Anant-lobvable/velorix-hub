# Download VeloRix Tournaments (Android)

VeloRix Tournaments is distributed as a free Android APK from this website. No website account is required to download, and there is no charge for the app itself.

- Platform: Android 7.0 (Nougat) or newer
- Size: approximately 25 MB
- Price: free
- Download page: [/download](/download)
- Machine-readable version info: MCP tool `get_active_apk` (see [/developers](/developers))

## Install steps

1. Open [/download](/download) on the Android device you play on.
2. Tap the download button and wait for the APK to finish downloading.
3. When Android asks, allow installation from your browser for this one install.
4. Open the app, create your player profile with your in-game name and UID.
5. Pick a Free Fire or BGMI tournament from the daily schedule and register.

## Notes

Only install VeloRix builds downloaded from this domain. Re-uploaded APKs from third-party mirrors are not verified by us and may be modified. If an install fails, free up storage, then retry; if it still fails, email service.veloxyra@gmail.com with your device model and Android version. Current platform health and download-service status is published on [/status](/status), and per-release notes are on [/changelog](/changelog).
