# Contact VeloRix Tournaments

VeloRix Tournaments support is handled by a small team based in India. Email is the primary and fastest channel; we do not run a phone support line. Please include your in-game name, the tournament name and the match time when reporting a match, payout or ban issue, because that is what lets us pull the correct lobby record.

## Support channels

- Email (all enquiries, support, payouts, appeals): service.veloxyra@gmail.com
- Instagram (announcements and quick questions): https://www.instagram.com/velorix_tournaments
- Service status and incident history: [/status](/status)
- Self-service answers: [/help](/help)

## What to email us about

- Tournament registration problems or missing room ID / password
- Disputed match results or scoring questions
- Cash prize payout status after a win
- Anti-cheat flags, bans and appeals
- Partnership, sponsorship and press enquiries
- Privacy requests (data access or deletion) — see [/privacy](/privacy)

## Response expectations

We aim to reply to support email within 24 to 48 hours. Payout and anti-cheat reviews can take longer when a match is under manual review, since a human watches the flagged clips before a result is finalised. If a tournament is affected by a platform-wide incident, the fastest source of truth is the status page rather than email.
