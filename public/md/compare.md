# How VeloRix Compares — Positioning

An honest description of where VeloRix sits among Indian mobile esports tournament platforms. Last reviewed: 2026-08-23. This page describes VeloRix's own choices; it makes no claims about any competitor's current pricing or policies.

## Category

VeloRix is a skill-based mobile esports tournament platform focused on India: Free Fire and BGMI first, with Call of Duty Mobile, Clash Squad and Valorant Mobile alongside. It competes in the same category as general Indian tournament apps and community-run custom-room organisers.

## Where VeloRix deliberately differs

- Free-entry tournaments run every day, so a new player never has to pay to evaluate the platform.
- Rules, scoring and prize distribution are published before registration opens, in writing, per tournament.
- Anti-cheat is a product priority, not an afterthought: automated flags plus mandatory human review of flagged matches before prizes release.
- Built and tested for budget Android phones rather than flagships; roughly 25 MB and Android 7.0+.
- Public transparency surfaces most tournament apps do not publish: a live status page, a public changelog, a read-only JSON API, and an MCP server for agents.
- No betting, wagering, fantasy staking or account trading of any kind.

## Where VeloRix is currently behind larger platforms

- Smaller player base and fewer concurrent lobbies than long-established Indian tournament apps.
- Android only; no iOS build.
- Fewer supported game titles than broad multi-game platforms.
- No web-based tournament joining: registration happens in the Android app.

## How to choose

Pick VeloRix if you want published rules, daily free-entry matches, strict anti-cheat review and a platform that runs well on a budget phone. Pick a larger platform if you mainly need very high concurrent lobby volume or titles VeloRix does not yet support.
