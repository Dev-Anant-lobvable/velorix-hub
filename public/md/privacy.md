# Privacy Policy — VeloRix Tournaments

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains what VeloRix Tournaments collects, why we collect it, and what control you have. It covers both the VeloRix website and the VeloRix Android app.

## What we collect

- Account data you provide in the app: in-game name, game UID, email address and, where required for prize payouts, a payment handle such as a UPI ID.
- Tournament data: registrations, match results, scores, screenshots or clips you submit, and anti-cheat review outcomes.
- Technical data: device model, Android version, app version, crash reports, IP address and coarse region. This is used for compatibility work and fraud prevention.
- Website analytics: aggregated page views and performance metrics. Non-essential cookies are only set after you accept them in the cookie banner.

## Why we use it

We use your data to run tournaments (registration, lobby access, scoring), to verify results and enforce fair play, to pay prizes to verified winners, to provide support, and to keep the platform secure against multi-accounting and fraud. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use your data for third-party advertising profiles.

## Sharing

Data is shared only with service providers that host our backend, deliver the app, process payouts, or provide analytics, and only to the extent needed to perform those functions. We may disclose data where required by Indian law or to investigate abuse of the platform.

## Retention and your rights

Tournament and payout records are retained as long as needed for dispute resolution and legal obligations; other data is deleted when it is no longer needed. You can request access to your data, correction of inaccurate data, or deletion of your account and associated personal data by emailing service.veloxyra@gmail.com. Deleting an account does not remove records of confirmed cheating.

## Children

VeloRix is not intended for users under 13. Users under 18 should have a parent or guardian review these terms before joining paid tournaments.
