VeloRix Tournaments — Free Fire & BGMI Tournament App for India

VeloRix Tournaments is an India-focused mobile esports platform and free Android app for Free Fire, BGMI, Call of Duty Mobile, Clash Squad and other competitive mobile titles. Players join daily custom-room tournaments, compete in structured formats such as points tables, brackets and round robin, and win real cash prizes paid after match verification. The app is free to download, needs no website account, and runs on Android 7.0 and newer at roughly 25 MB — deliberately built light so it stays usable on budget and mid-range phones and on slow mobile connections.

How it works

  1. Download the VeloRix Android app from the download page.
  2. Pick a Free Fire or BGMI tournament from the daily schedule.
  3. Register and receive the custom room ID and password before match time.
  4. Play the match; results pass in-house anti-cheat plus manual review of flagged games.
  5. Verified winners receive cash prizes once the review is complete.

Why players use VeloRix

  • Daily Free Fire, BGMI, Clash Squad and Call of Duty Mobile tournaments.
  • Real cash prizes paid after match verification, with a record of the decision.
  • In-house anti-cheat with human review of flagged matches, bans and an appeals path.
  • Built light so it runs well on budget Android phones and weak connections.
  • Free-entry tournaments alongside paid prize pools.

FAQ

Is the app free?

Yes. The app is free to download and many tournaments have free entry.

Which games are supported?

Free Fire, BGMI, Call of Duty Mobile, Clash Squad and more.

How do payouts work?

Cash prizes are paid to verified winners after the match result is reviewed by our team.

Is VeloRix a betting or gambling app?

No. VeloRix runs skill-based esports competitions only; no outcome depends on chance.
